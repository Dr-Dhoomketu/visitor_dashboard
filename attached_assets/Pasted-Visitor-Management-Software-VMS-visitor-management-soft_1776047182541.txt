Visitor Management Software (VMS)
visitor management software offers organizations a modern, efficient, and secure
way to manage and monitor the flow of visitors into their facilities. It helps
improve security, compliance, and the overall visitor experience while reducing
administrative tasks associated with visitor registration. Different software
providers offer various features, so organizations can choose a solution that aligns
with their specific needs and requirements.
The purpose of visitor management software is to efficiently and securely manage
visitors entering a business, organization, or facility. It serves several key purposes:
1. Enhanced Security: Visitor management software helps enhance the security
of a facility by tracking who enters and exits, which can be critical for ensuring
that only authorized individuals gain access. It can also check visitors against
watchlists or databases to identify potential security threats.
2. Improved Safety: In addition to security, visitor management systems can
contribute to safety by keeping a record of who is in the building at any given
time. This can be essential for emergency evacuations or contact tracing during
health crises.
3. Efficiency: Visitor management software reduces the administrative burden
associated with manual visitor registration and tracking. This frees up staff time
for other tasks and can help organizations operate more efficiently.
4. Compliance: Many industries and organizations are subject to regulatory
requirements that mandate visitor tracking and record-keeping. Visitor
management software helps ensure compliance with these regulations by
automatically maintaining visitor records.
5. Professional Image: Using modern technology for visitor registration conveys
a professional image of the organization. It shows that the organization values
security, efficiency, and the visitor experience.
6. Data Analysis: The data collected by visitor management systems can be
analyzed to gain insights into visitor patterns, peak visit times, and other
valuable information. This data can be used to optimize staffing, security
measures, and facility layouts.
7. Access Control Integration: Some visitor management solutions integrate
with access control systems, allowing authorized visitors to access specific
areas or receive temporary access credentials.
8. Visitor Accountability: By providing visitor badges or passes, the software
holds visitors accountable for their actions during their stay and helps identify
them in the event of any incidents.
9. Visitor Experience: A smooth, organized check-in process contributes to a
positive visitor experience, which can be especially important for businesses
focused on customer service or organizations hosting events or conferences.
 Here is a Home Page of visitor management software.
 On this page, you'll find three options:-
a) Schedule an Appointment
b) Previous Visits Log
c) Log In/Check In
 When you click on the 'Log-in/Check-in' option, an animated page opens. Upon
selecting the 'Check-in' option on the left side of this page, a corresponding
section or page is displayed below
 Here is a Check-in Form
 In this page, please fill in the required details, including your Name,
National ID (Aadhar Card No), Gender, the person you wish to meet
(Meet With), Phone Number, Email, Purpose of your visit, and Address.
Once you have completed the form, click the 'Check-in' option. After
clicking 'Check-in,' the webcam page will open.
 Here is a Webcam page
 Here you can captured image of an visitor .
 After this process, an email is generated and sent to the intended person,
notifying them of the visitor's presence and desire to meet.
 Here is a Approval page
 Once the employee approves the visitor, the individual is granted permission
to meet with the designated person from this page.
 On this page, 'Pending' displays the number of pending requests, 'Approved'
indicates the total number of approved requests, and 'Rejected' shows the
count of rejected requests.
 The list below represents the Visitor List, where requests can be approved or
rejected. Once any action, be it approval or rejection, is performed, the
respective buttons are disabled to prevent further actions. Subsequently, an
email notification is sent to the visitor informing them of the approval or
rejection.
 Here is Have an Appointment Page.
 This is the appointment page where you can enter basic
information. After completing this step, the visitor will receive an
email confirming the successful booking of their appointment.
 Here is Been Here Before
 This page is designed for returning visitors. You can enter their
mobile number to retrieve all relevant information and provide
details about the purpose of their visit.
 Here is a login page
 Here, you can input your user ID and password to log in.
 Here is a Dashboard
 In the dashboard, the 'Total Visitor' metric signifies the overall count of
visitors we have welcomed. Similarly, 'Today’s Visitors' reflects the total
number of visitors received today, 'Total Check-out' indicates the number
of visitors who have completed their visit and checked out, while 'Total
Check-in' signifies the count of visitors who have successfully checked
in. Additionally, the 'Pre-Visitor' metric represents the number of
employees who have booked appointments in advance.
 Below this, you'll find a list of approved visitors, each accompanied by a
unique visitor ID. This ID specifically identifies those whose visitor
requests have been successfully approved.
 Here is a Department Page.
 You can add new departments by selecting the 'Add Department' option.
Below, there is a list of existing departments displayed.
 In the department list, you'll find the Department ID, Department Name,
and their status, which can be either 'Active' or 'Inactive.' For each
department, In Action you have the option to 'Delete' or 'Edit' it.
 Here is a Designation Page.
 You can add new Designation by selecting the 'Add Department' option.
Below, there is a list of existing Designation displayed.
 In the department list, you'll find the Designation ID, Designation Name,
and their status, which can be either 'Active' or 'Inactive.' For each
designation, In Action you have the option to 'Delete' or 'Edit' it.
 Here is a Employee Page.
 You can add New Employee by selecting the 'Add Employee' option.
Below, there is a list of existing Employee displayed.
 In the Employee list, you'll find the Employee ID, Employee Name,
Employee E-mail, Employee Phone Number, and their status, which can
be either 'Active' or 'Inactive.' In Action you have the option to 'Delete'
or 'Edit' it.
 Here is a Visitor Page.
 Below, you'll find a comprehensive list of existing visitors,
organized by the days they were recorded.
 Here is a Pre-visitor Page
 This is a pre-visitor Report page that allows you to search for Report lists
based on specific dates. Once you enter your search criteria, you will receive
the pre-visitor list that matches your search.
 Here is a User Page Under Administrator Option
In the Administrator page you can add Users .
 You can add New User by selecting the 'Add User’ option. Below, there
is a list of existing Users displayed.
 In the User list, you'll find the User ID, User Name, User E-mail ,
Department , Designation and their Action option to 'delete' or 'Edit' it.
